Model & limitations

Model: MediaPipe Face Mesh (468 landmarks + iris refinement), with a deterministic geometric heuristic for gaze direction and expression, both documented in MLOPS_NOTES.md.

  • Single face only; additional faces in frame are ignored.
  • Sensitive to poor lighting, extreme head angles, and occlusion (glasses, masks, hair).
  • Gaze/expression labels are coarse heuristics, not a trained or clinically validated classifier.
  • Requires HTTPS or localhost for camera access (browser security requirement).
